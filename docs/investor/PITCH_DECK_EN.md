❌ **6-month dev cycle** — most teams can't wait and can't pull it off
❌ **Ongoing ops nightmare** — accounts get banned in batches, nobody wants the job
❌ **AI/sales disconnect** — ChatGPT wrappers can't link user profiles or collaborate with sales

## ✨ SOLUTION

**From $199/month, 5-minute setup. Platform supplies the entire infrastructure + AI lead-gen capability.**

```
Customer logs in → picks industry → platform auto-customizes accounts+personas+KB
  → joins 500-3000 target groups → AI runs 7×24 lead-gen
  → high-intent alerts to sales for Copilot-assisted closing
```
