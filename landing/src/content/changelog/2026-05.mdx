## 2026-05 — AI marketing assistant goes GA

This month's highlight: every paid customer now has the AI marketing
assistant enabled by default. Configure a monitor rule, drop in your
target groups, and AI handles first-touch in-group while your sales
seat owns the DM. The half-automatic boundary is enforced at the API
layer — `reply_mode=private_dm` is hard-rejected for customer-owned
rules, by design.

### New

- **Customer-owned monitor rules** at `/portal/monitors`. Authors a
  rule scoped to your tenant; the listener applies a third filter
  (after the Phase G sales-ownership check) so monitors only fire
  on your accounts.
- **Sales-owned `marketing_mode='active'`** is now allowed (was
  hard-rejected). Reply mode is forced to `group_reply`; daily limit
  defaults to 20 per rule to bound ban risk.
- **Auto-enable AI marketing slugs on subscription activation**. The
  gate (`ai_marketing_assistant`) + per-event slugs
  (`ai_marketing_group_reply`, `ai_marketing_lead_created`) all flip
  to `enabled=True` when a customer activates any plan. Stops the
  silent-free-service bug we caught in smoke.
- **Lead pre-assignment now covers CustomerUser seats**, not just
  platform sales (`assigned_to_sales_kind='customer'`). Your sales
  team sees AI-captured leads pre-pinned to them on first inbox load.

### Portal UX

- **Portal-wide low-balance banner**. Any portal page (except wallet
  itself) shows a red banner when wallet < $20, with a one-click
  topup link. Hides on `/portal/wallet` to avoid double-warning.
- **Monthly wallet report**, including `/customer/wallet/report.csv`
  export. Six source buckets (scrape / bulk_send / invite / ai_marketing
  / other_feature / other) inferred from idempotency keys.

### Performance

- **BGE cross-encoder reranker** landed (`fastembed` ONNX, opt-in via
  `RERANK_ENABLED=true`). On dev queries we see ~12pt recall gain on
  the top-3 retrieved KB chunks.

### Bug fixes

- `conftest.py` now imports `app.models` package eagerly so
  `SQLModel.metadata.create_all()` builds every table. Prior to the
  fix, `tests/test_api_accounts.py` had 15 fixture-level errors and
  0 passes; now 43/46 pass.
- Tier was being persisted from a stale role on tdata-import races.
  `[ALC] retire manual tier picker` derives tier from role at every
  write point, removing the redundant manual UI.
