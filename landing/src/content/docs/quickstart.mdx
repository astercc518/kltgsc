# Quickstart

This is the 15-minute path from sign-up to your first AI-captured lead.
If you only read one doc, read this one.

## 1. Register + claim your $20 trial

Go to [https://tg1.ai/portal/register?trial=20](https://tg1.ai/portal/register?trial=20).
Use your work email. The trial credit lands in your wallet immediately —
no card, no chain transfer required.

> You're now an active tenant on the Starter feature set. You have 3 TG
> accounts pre-allocated, 500 group slots, 2M token budget, 1 sales seat.

## 2. Bind your main account

Go to **Portal → My Telegram** and scan the QR code from your phone.
This main account is what AI uses to push lead notifications to your
Saved Messages — handy during onboarding, optional later.

The system never asks for your password. We use Telegram's official
QR-login flow, encrypted at rest.

## 3. Upload a Knowledge Base

**Portal → Knowledge Base → New KB**. Drop in a PDF / markdown of your
product description, FAQ, or top sales objections. The platform indexes
it into a 768-dim pgvector store using Vertex Gemini embeddings.

AI replies will cite your KB on intent matches (paraphrased — never
copy-pasted). One KB takes ~10 seconds to embed per MB.

## 4. Create a monitor rule

**Portal → AI Listener → New rule**. Pick:

- **Keyword(s)** — comma-separated, language-agnostic
- **Target groups** — paste @handles or invite links (required, so you
  never monitor "the whole world")
- **Mode** — `passive` (record hits only) or `active` (AI replies in-group)
- **Persona** — pick one your KB cites well

⚠️ Customer-owned rules can **only** reply in-group. `reply_mode=private_dm`
is hard-rejected. AI never auto-DMs your prospects — that's your sales
team's job. See [Billing rules](./billing) for why.

## 5. Wait for a hit

The listener fires on each new message in your target groups. Two-stage
match: keyword pre-filter → Gemini semantic judge. When confidence
clears your threshold, a `KeywordHit` row is recorded, and if the rule
is `active`, AI posts a contextual reply.

A `Lead` row is created automatically, pre-assigned to whoever owns
that TG account. Cost: **$0.05** for the AI reply + **$0.50** for the
lead — both metered from your wallet on success only.

## 6. Take over the conversation

Open **Sales Workbench → Inbox**. New leads land at the top. One click
on **Claim** transitions the lead to your name — AI switches to "draft
mode" (suggestions in the side pane, no auto-send). You DM the prospect
manually using your bound account.

After takeover, the conversation logs to **CRM**. Unconverted leads
stay there for follow-up.
