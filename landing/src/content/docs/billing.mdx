# Billing rules

The product runs on a **two-layer** billing model. Subscription is
the recurring infrastructure cost (accounts, group slots, token
budget, sales seats). Wallet is the variable cost (per-action,
prepaid).

If you remember only one sentence: **subscription unlocks ability,
wallet meters use.**

## Unit prices (variable, wallet-metered)

Every action that hits Telegram or invokes an LLM is metered. Admin
can override any line per-customer for volume deals — talk to sales.

| Product | Action | Billed as | Default |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Self-Serve | Group scrape          | per member  | **$0.01** |
| Self-Serve | Bulk send             | per message | **$0.10** |
| Self-Serve | Bulk invite           | per invite  | **$0.05** |
| AI Assistant | AI group reply      | per reply   | **$0.05** |
| AI Assistant | AI auto-lead        | per lead    | **$0.50** |
| AI Assistant | Sales lead view     | per view    | **$0.10** |
| KB | File embed                  | per MB      | **$0.20** |

Costs include the AI tokens needed for that action — you do not pay
LLM tokens separately on the wallet. Your subscription's monthly token
budget is a soft envelope; we'll alert you (not bill) if you cross it.

## "Half-automatic" AI

Customer-owned monitors and sales-owned monitors can both fire
`marketing_mode='active'` — meaning AI generates a reply automatically.
**But the reply only ever posts in the source group**, never as a
private DM.

The API rejects `reply_mode='private_dm'` for any non-admin owner.
This is a deliberate safety contract:

- Ban risk on Telegram is concentrated in unsolicited DMs.
- In-group replies look the same as any other group member chiming in.
- Your sales team should DM the prospect after takeover.

You can read more about why in the
[product positioning blog post](/changelog) — but practically, this
is the line that keeps your TG accounts alive.

## Auto-pause on low wallet

When your wallet balance drops below the cost of the **next** task
unit, the platform transitions in-flight tasks to `paused_no_funds`.

- Already-issued ops (in-flight scrape rows, queued bulk targets)
  continue until each one's idempotency key has been consumed.
- New ops stop. No retries, no exponential backoff storm, no
  duplicate charges on resume.
- Sales seats still see existing leads in their inbox. Just no
  new ones get pre-assigned until you top up.

When you top up, paused tasks resume from where they left off.

## Refunds

We don't issue refunds for wallet balance — it never debits unless
you actually used the service. Subscription refunds within the
first 7 days are pro-rated and processed back to your USDT address.
After day 7, sub is non-refundable but you can downgrade or cancel
at any time (no auto-renewal trap).

## Auditing

Every wallet transaction has an idempotency key encoding what it
paid for: `feat:bulk_send_message:42:1024` is "bulk_send_message
for batch 42, target 1024". Export your full history any month as
CSV from the **Portal → Wallet → 月度报表 → 导出 CSV** button or
via [GET /customer/wallet/report.csv](./api).
