# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-25._

TG1.AI ("we", "us") operates the tg1.ai platform. This page describes
what data we collect, why, how long we keep it, and what choices you
have. It's deliberately short — if you want the legal-form version
with the IRAC headings, email [privacy@tg1.ai](mailto:privacy@tg1.ai)
and we'll send it.

## Who's the controller?

Customer data (everything you upload, scrape, or store on the platform)
is **yours**. We are the **processor**. You are the controller. If
your end users have rights under GDPR / CCPA / PIPL, those rights flow
to you, not us; we provide the tooling for you to respond.

For the marketing site itself — the landing you're reading right now —
TG1.AI is the controller of the data we collect (page views, language
selection, conversion events).

## What we collect on the landing site

| Source | Field | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Plausible analytics | URL path, referrer, country, browser, device class | Did the redesign actually improve conversion? No cookies, no fingerprint. |
| localStorage | `tg1_lang` | Remember your language between visits. |
| Signup form | email, password (hashed) | Create your tenant. |

We don't run Google Analytics. We don't run Facebook Pixel. We don't
share visitor data with anyone outside our infrastructure.

## What we collect from your tenant (the actual product)

- **Session strings + tdata blobs** — encrypted at rest with AES-256
  using a per-instance KEK. Used by Pyrogram to talk to Telegram on
  your behalf.
- **Group messages your accounts can see** — only on monitored
  channels; stored as `KeywordHit` and `GroupMessage` rows scoped to
  your `customer_id`.
- **Lead conversations** — DMs your accounts have with prospects,
  for the duration you have the lead. Logged to CRM at close.
- **Usage records** — every wallet transaction with idempotency key
  encoding what it paid for. Used for billing + your CSV export.
- **LLM call traces** — input prompt + output reply for every AI
  reply or rewrite, with token counts. Used for cost attribution
  and quality improvements.

Everything tagged with `customer_id` — the listener, the API, the RAG
retrieval, and the sales workbench all enforce row-level filters.

## How long do we keep it?

- Active tenant data: while your subscription is active.
- 30 days after cancel: cold storage in case of dispute / reactivation.
- After day 30: deleted. We do not sell archived customer data.

You can request immediate deletion at
[privacy@tg1.ai](mailto:privacy@tg1.ai) with a signed message from
the email used to register.

## Subprocessors

- **Vertex AI (Google Cloud)** — LLM completions + 768-dim
  embeddings. Data processed in-region; not used to train Google
  models per their TOS.
- **NowPayments** — invoice processing. Forwards tx_hash callbacks
  via webhook. We never see your private keys.
- **Plausible Analytics (EU)** — landing site visitor metrics.
- **Hetzner / VPS provider** — server hosting. Customer data lives
  on encrypted volumes.

## Your rights

- Access — export everything via `/customer/wallet/report.csv` plus a
  full data-export request.
- Rectification — edit any record via the portal.
- Erasure — see "How long" above.
- Portability — CSV exports are CSV; the format is documented in
  [api docs](/docs/api).
