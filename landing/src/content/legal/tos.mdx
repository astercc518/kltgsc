## 3. What we do (and don't do)

We do:

- Maintain ≥99.9% uptime SLA on Growth + Pro tiers.
- Replace banned accounts per the SLA in your plan (24 h Starter /
  12 h Growth / 4 h Pro).
- Encrypt your session strings + tdata at rest with AES-256.
- Enforce the half-automatic boundary — AI never auto-DMs your
  prospects, in code, at the API layer.
- Enforce tenant isolation in the listener + API + RAG retrieval.

We don't:

- Custody funds. USDT goes to your tenant's payment address; we
  never hold your money.
- Send messages without your account. Every outbound action is
  authored by one of your Telegram accounts via Pyrogram.
- Read your DMs unless you've opted in to logging them to CRM
  (which is the default on Lead claim, but you can disable per-lead).
- Train AI models on your data. Vertex Gemini calls are billed as
  inference, never as training contributions.
